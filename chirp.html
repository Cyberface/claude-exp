How scoring works

Your microphone is analysed about 60 times a second to find the pitch you're singing. For every moment of the 4-second chirp, the closer your pitch is to the target curve, the more accuracy you earn: a perfect match is 100%, and you get nothing once you're 1.5 semitones away. Silence counts as zero, so you need to sing the whole way through. A round is worth up to 1000 points.

"Forgive octave slips" folds your pitch by whole octaves before comparing, so singing the same note an octave high or low still counts. Turn it off if you want a stricter challenge. Your microphone audio never leaves your browser.
